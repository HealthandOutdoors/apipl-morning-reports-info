APIpl Morning Reports

APIpl Morning Reports is a private Windows application used by Health and Outdoors to prepare and email its internal daily and rolling seven-day profit-and-loss reports.

The application runs on the owner's computer. It requests report refreshes through the owner's existing APIpl and APIstream services, checks report completion, and sends the resulting Excel workbooks to the owner's configured business email address. An optional Google Sheets connection also copies the SKU P&L worksheet from each verified report into two dedicated tabs selected by the owner.

Google connection

The owner connects a Google account so the application can send report emails through Gmail. The Gmail connection requests send-only permission and does not request permission to read, search, or delete inbox messages. Optional Google Sheets access uses a separate authorization. Google grants spreadsheet access for the connected account; application safeguards restrict writes to the configured workbook and two report tab IDs. Other tabs and sharing settings are not modified. There is no public signup or downloadable service on this website.

Reports and credentials are not published on this website. Google authorization is completed directly with Google.
