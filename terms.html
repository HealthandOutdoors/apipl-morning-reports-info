APIpl Morning Reports Terms of Use

Effective October 14, 2026.

APIpl Morning Reports is an internal reporting utility for Health and Outdoors and its authorized operator. It is not offered as a public subscription service, and this informational page does not create an account or charge a fee.

The authorized operator controls the connected Google account, report recipient, schedule, and local installation. The application sends reports and operational notices for that configured business purpose.

Report results depend on available source data and business inputs. The operator should review report coverage, exclusions, and calculations before using results for business decisions. The morning-report module does not change marketplace prices or inventory.

The operator may stop the worker, disable the schedule, or revoke the Google connection to discontinue automated sending.
