APIpl Morning Reports Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. This policy covers the private APIpl Morning Reports application operated by Health and Outdoors. It does not replace the store's shopping privacy policy.

Information the application handles

The application handles the owner-configured sender and recipient email addresses, Google OAuth authorization credentials, report email text and Excel attachments, and the message identifier returned by Google after sending. Reports may contain confidential business and order information obtained from the owner's existing reporting services. When optional Google Sheets publication is enabled, the application also handles the configured workbook and report tab identifiers, report-tab values and formatting, prior-tab snapshots, and verification receipts.

Google permissions and purpose

The application requests Gmail send-only access to deliver the owner's reports and operational notices. It does not request access to read, search, or delete inbox messages. OAuth credentials let the scheduled worker send authorized emails without requiring a new sign-in each morning.

Optional Google Sheets publication uses a separate authorization with the spreadsheets permission. Google grants access to spreadsheets available to the connected account. The application limits its writes to the owner's configured workbook and two dedicated report tabs, and reads those tabs to verify publication. It copies the SKU P&L worksheet from the verified daily and rolling seven-day reports. It does not modify other tabs, change sharing settings, or make the workbook public.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are encrypted on the owner's Windows computer using Windows Data Protection API. Local report files, settings, and delivery records are stored on that computer. Report email content and attachments are sent through Google to the owner's configured business email recipient. Delivered messages and attachments remain subject to the sender's and recipient's email services and retention settings.

When Google Sheets publication is enabled, Google receives report-tab content for storage in the selected spreadsheet. That content remains subject to the spreadsheet's existing sharing permissions and Google's service settings. Prior-tab snapshots and verification records are stored locally on the owner's computer. The Gmail and Sheets credentials are stored separately.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence models. It does not share Google user data with unrelated parties. The public informational pages do not contain the owner's reports or credentials.

APIpl Morning Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Local authorization credentials remain until the owner removes or replaces the connection. Local reports, prior-tab snapshots, and verification and delivery records follow the application's retention settings and the owner's file-management choices. The owner can stop the worker, disable publication, and revoke access at any time through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not remove already-delivered emails or previously published report tabs. The owner can request assistance removing local app data using the support contact shown on the Google authorization screen.

This informational website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They do not provide a sign-in form or upload facility and add no analytics scripts. GitHub may collect technical visitor information as described in its privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about this private application or its privacy practices, use the support contact shown on the Google authorization screen.
